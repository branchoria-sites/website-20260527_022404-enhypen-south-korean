## Introduction

For fans, ENHYPEN are more than a group with a vampire concept. Their strongest releases use fantasy to express recognisable experiences: entering an unfamiliar world, questioning whether success was earned, losing control amid attention, and wanting connection without surrendering one’s identity. That continuity has helped them grow from survival-show rookies into a major touring and album-selling act whose releases have repeatedly reached the upper end of the US Billboard 200.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[beliftlab.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">IMENSION: DILEMMA which unraveled ENHYPEN’s story beyond the border of debut where they realized their new world was even more complex a...</span></span></span>

## How ENHYPEN were formed

ENHYPEN emerged from *I-LAND*, a global trainee competition broadcast by Mnet in 2020. The programme followed contestants through performances, evaluations and public voting, giving viewers a direct role in creating the final group. BELIFT LAB describes the project as a global artist-development initiative, an origin that explains why ENHYPEN possessed an international audience even before their official debut.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/profile/ENHYPEN?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BELIFT LAB빌리프랩 &#124; BELIFT LABNovember 30, 2020 — ENHYPEN—consisting of JUNGWON, JAY, JAKE, SUNGHOON, SUNOO, and NI-KI—are a six-member mult...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 30, 2020</span></span></span>

The name ENHYPEN refers to the connecting function of a hyphen. The intended idea is that separate people can connect, discover one another and grow into something new. That principle also describes the relationship between the group and its official fandom, ENGENE, whose name evokes both an “engine” driving the group forward and the shared “genes” through which artists and fans connect. BELIFT LAB formally announced the fandom name and membership programme shortly before the group’s debut.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://weverse.io/enhypen/notice/709" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: weverse.io">[Weverse]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">weverse.io</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on weverse.io.</span></span></span>

Their debut EP, *Border: Day One*, arrived on 30 November 2020. Its lead single, “[Given-Taken]({{ 'given-taken/' | relative_url }})”, immediately established the central tension in their early work: had the members been given the opportunity to debut, or had they taken it through effort and competition? The song paired that uncertainty with dark pop production and supernatural visual imagery, introducing the combination of personal anxiety and fantasy storytelling that would become the group’s signature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.grammy.com/news/enhypen-interview-border-day-one-k-pop/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: grammy.com">[Grammy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">grammy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Sunoo: I like &quot;Let Me In (20 CUBE)&quot; because of its catchiness, but &quot;10 Months&quot; resonated with me the most, because the lyrics [say] that...</span></span></span>

## Who the members are

The current line-up mixes Korean, Korean-American, Australian-raised and Japanese backgrounds, reinforcing the international identity present from the group’s formation. BELIFT LAB’s official profile lists Jungwon, Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo and Ni-Ki as the six present members.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/profile/ENHYPEN?lang=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">BELIFT LAB빌리프랩 &#124; BELIFT LABNovember 30, 2020 — ENHYPEN—consisting of JUNGWON, JAY, JAKE, SUNGHOON, SUNOO, and NI-KI—are a six-member mult...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 30, 2020</span></span></span>

* **Jungwon** is the group’s leader. His controlled dancing, clear vocal tone and composed manner frequently place him at the centre of transitions between ENHYPEN’s sharper and more melodic material.
* **Jay** is known for his forceful stage presence, expressive delivery and interest in instruments and songwriting.
* **Jake**, who grew up in Australia, often brings a warm, conversational quality to interviews and English-language promotions.
* **Sunghoon** trained for years as a competitive figure skater before becoming an idol. His skating background is visible in his balance, lines and precision; in 2026, he carried the Winter Olympics torch in Milan, linking his former sporting ambitions with his public career.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://people.com/enhypen-sunghoon-2026-winter-olympics-torchbearer-exclusive-photos-11908430" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: people.com">[People.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">people.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">In the interview, Sunghoon discusses his emotional Olympic experience, including supporting Team Korea and reminiscing about his skating...</span></span></span>
* **Sunoo** provides a bright but highly adaptable performance style, moving convincingly between playful material and the group’s darker concepts.
* **Ni-Ki**, the Japanese youngest member, is especially recognised for dance, physical control and a commanding presence in performance-focused tracks.

Heeseung was an original member and one of the group’s principal vocalists. BELIFT LAB announced his departure in March 2026, stating that ENHYPEN and Heeseung would begin separate chapters. He subsequently debuted as the soloist Evan in June 2026. The change marked the first alteration to the group’s line-up and inevitably reshaped vocal distribution and performance arrangements developed over the preceding five years.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://weverse.io/enhypen/notice/34159" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: weverse.io">[weverse.io]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">weverse.io</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Weverse[NOTICE] ENHYPEN&#x27;s Future Activities# [NOTICE] ENHYPEN&#x27;s Future Activities... ## The songs and eras that define them

ENHYPEN’s catalogue works best as a sequence of related chapters rather than disconnected comeback concepts. The early *Border* releases present debut as the crossing of a threshold. *Border: Carnival* then examines the disorientation of suddenly becoming the focus of cheers and attention. “[Drunk-Dazed]({{ 'drunk-dazed/' | relative_url }})” turns fame into an overwhelming party from which the participants cannot easily escape, while “[FEVER]({{ 'fever/' | relative_url }})” slows the mood into a restrained, hypnotic expression of desire.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on beliftlab.com.</span></span></span>

The *Dimension* era moves from arrival to uncertainty. On *Dimension: Dilemma*, the members encounter competing values and desires in the world beyond debut. Songs such as “Tamed-Dashed” and “[Blessed-Cursed]({{ 'blessed-cursed/' | relative_url }})” frame maturity as a choice between accepting imposed expectations and defining a path independently. BELIFT LAB’s official album description explicitly connects this period with questions of identity in a newly complex environment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/4" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">IMENSION: DILEMMA which unraveled ENHYPEN’s story beyond the border of debut where they realized their new world was even more complex a...</span></span></span>

*Manifesto: Day 1* made that rejection more confrontational. Its title track, “[Future Perfect]({{ 'future-perfect/' | relative_url }}) (Pass the MIC)”, prioritised heavy rhythm and collective declaration over the dreamier atmosphere of earlier releases. The album reworked ENHYPEN’s recurring theme of connection: rather than accepting a future handed down by others, the members present themselves as participants in creating it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/5" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on beliftlab.com.</span></span></span>

The *Blood* series became the clearest expression of their supernatural identity. *Dark Blood* asked what happens when people who are bound together lose sight of one another. Its lead single, “[Bite Me]({{ 'bite-me/' | relative_url }})”, used restrained hooks, paired [choreography]({{ 'choreography/' | relative_url }}) and vampire imagery to portray an intimate but dangerous bond. *Orange Blood* continued the story with a warmer palette, shifting attention towards mortality, devotion and the desire to remain connected after recognising that permanence cannot be guaranteed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Carefully crafted with ENHYPEN&#x27;s one-of-kind unique concept and quality music, DARK BLOOD is sure to become the album listeners are desti...</span></span></span>

In 2024, *Romance: Untold* placed loyalty and romantic commitment at the centre of the group’s story. “XO (Only If You Say Yes)” softened ENHYPEN’s usual menace with bright synth-pop, while tracks such as “Brought the Heat Back” retained the theatrical tension of their darker work. The album was also notable for deeper member participation: Circle Chart reported that all seven members contributed to lyrics, composition or production.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/10" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on beliftlab.com.</span></span></span>

The subsequent releases *Desire: Unleash* and *The Sin: Vanish* pushed the narrative towards longing, temptation and transgression. BELIFT LAB described *Desire: Unleash* as an exploration of yearning to stand as an equal beside the person one loves, while *The Sin: Vanish* began a more immersive narrative series that incorporated songs, spoken narration and dramatic interludes. In August 2026, the group is scheduled to continue this sequence with the eighth EP *The Sin: Bliss*.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/14" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on beliftlab.com.</span></span></span>

## Why the vampire story matters


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

Vampire imagery is common in popular culture, but ENHYPEN have used it with unusual consistency. It offers a flexible language for discussing adolescence and celebrity: transformation is exciting but irreversible; desire can create intimacy or dependence; eternal life resembles both devotion and entrapment; and belonging to a group can provide strength while threatening individuality.

</div>

This mythology extends beyond music videos. The group’s releases are connected to the *Dark Moon* fictional universe, in which supernatural characters and relationships echo themes from the albums. The result is not a perfectly linear plot that every listener must decode. Instead, the lore supplies recurring symbols—blood, invitations, mirrors, fate, memory and forbidden connection—that reward fans who enjoy interpretation without making the songs inaccessible to casual listeners.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://beliftlab.com/artist/discography/ENHYPEN/6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: beliftlab.com">[BELIFT LAB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">beliftlab.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Carefully crafted with ENHYPEN&#x27;s one-of-kind unique concept and quality music, DARK BLOOD is sure to become the album listeners are desti...</span></span></span>

The concept succeeds because the strongest songs do not rely on lore alone. “Given-Taken” works as a debut song about legitimacy, “Drunk-Dazed” as a portrayal of overstimulation, “FEVER” as sensual pop and “Bite Me” as a compact statement of mutual dependence. The fantasy heightens emotions that are already understandable without supplementary material.

## Performance and choreography

ENHYPEN’s reputation is closely tied to coordinated movement. Their choreography favours sharp formation changes, low body positions, sudden shifts in tempo and gestures that serve the song’s narrative. In “Bite Me”, partner-based staging reinforces ideas of invitation and surrender. “Drunk-Dazed” uses constant motion to evoke instability, while “Future Perfect” relies on heavier, more grounded movement suited to its defiant rhythm.

Their live development became especially visible through international touring and festival appearances. At KCON Los Angeles in 2024, they combined darker tracks such as “Bite Me” with the lighter “Polaroid Love” and an English version of “XO”, illustrating how their set lists move between theatrical intensity and approachable pop.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.grammy.com/news/kcon-2024-performances-highlights-nct-127-nmixx-zerobaseone/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: grammy.com">[Grammy]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">grammy.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">kcon 2024 performances highlights nct 127 nmixx zerobaseone</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">kcon 2024 performances highlights nct 127 nmixx zerobaseone</span></span></span>

## How large their success has become

ENHYPEN’s commercial growth is most evident in album sales. *Romance: Untold* topped Circle Chart’s album ranking with approximately 1.84 million copies recorded in its release period, while its repackage, *Romance: Untold –daydream–*, later entered the chart with more than 1.12 million copies. Circle Chart also lists *Romance: Untold* among albums certified for at least one million cumulative sales.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://circlechart.kr/page_article/view.circle?idx=24056&amp;sgenre=kpop" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: circlechart.kr">[circlechart.kr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">circlechart.kr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on circlechart.kr.</span></span></span>

The group have also developed a consistent US chart presence. *Romance: Untold* debuted at number two on the Billboard 200 in July 2024 and led the Top Album Sales chart. Subsequent releases, including *Desire: Unleash* in 2025 and *The Sin: Vanish* in 2026, likewise reached number two on the Billboard 200. These results show unusually durable album demand rather than reliance on one crossover single.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ca.billboard.com/charts/billboard-200/2024" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ca.billboard.com">[Billboard Canada]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ca.billboard.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on billboard.com.</span></span></span>

That distinction matters. ENHYPEN’s international strength has been built principally through albums, concerts, physical collecting and a highly organised global fandom. Songs such as “Polaroid Love”, “FEVER”, “Bite Me” and “XO” have extended their reach, but the group’s broader success is rooted in listeners following each era as part of an evolving body of work.

## What distinguishes ENHYPEN

ENHYPEN occupy a recognisable place in modern K-pop because their core elements reinforce one another. The choreography expresses the tension in the songs; the supernatural imagery gives continuity to changing genres; and the lyrics repeatedly return to the emotional consequences of ambition, connection and transformation.

Their catalogue is varied—moving through dark pop, synth-pop, hip-hop-influenced tracks, dance music and softer fan songs—but it maintains a coherent emotional world. The group can make a bright track such as “Polaroid Love” without abandoning the sincerity that underpins its darker releases, and it can introduce heavy fantasy imagery without requiring every fan to treat the story as a puzzle.

The transition to six members is a significant break in ENHYPEN’s history, particularly because the original line-up had remained intact from *I-LAND* through five years of releases and tours. Yet the group’s identity has never depended on one device or one era. Its lasting foundation is the idea contained in its name: separate individuals becoming connected, changing through that connection and continually renegotiating what they will become next.
